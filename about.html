About Vision Sneakers

Class project. Real teen energy.

Our Story

Vision Sneakers is a simple sneaker brand created for a web design class. Instead of building a fake luxury brand, we imagined something closer to real life. We focused on students who want good-looking shoes that don’t cost a fortune.

Everything on this site is a prototype like the prices, the images, and the “brand”. The main goal was to practice HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript while still making a site that feels clean and usable.

What We Care About

Comfort
Our fake brand still cares about real feet. Cushion, flexibility, and support come first.
Expression
Bright colors, chunky soles, and simple shapes give room for personal style.
Accessibility
We imagine pricing that fits a part-time job or student budget.
